# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

FinEx Careers serves Hong Kong finance job seekers, especially university graduates and early-career candidates, as well as experienced finance professionals. Employers and recruiters use a separate account flow to submit roles.

## Product Purpose

FinEx Careers brings Hong Kong career opportunities, career consultation, and professional learning into one public portal. The Management Trainee directory helps graduates discover structured programmes across industries and understand which application deadlines need attention.

## Positioning

The product combines a daily, AI-enriched Hong Kong finance Role index with the Financial Executive Club's consultation and learning network. The Management Trainee prototype extends that discovery experience beyond ordinary job listings into an editorially curated programme directory.

## Operating Context

Seekers browse publicly on desktop and mobile, move between the portal, Careers board, Management Trainee directory, consultation, and learning, and may create an account to save Roles or use resume-based features.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 19, Vite, TypeScript, and Tailwind 4 frontend served by FastAPI.
- The Management Trainee work is currently a design and functionality prototype.
- The updated directory contains 73 companies across eight industries.
- The working directory supplies 130 usable programme URLs for 63 employers. Two official programme URLs are directly verified (HKJC and HKMA); application deadlines must not be invented.
- Management Trainee cards must make deadline status prominent and provide a route to career coaching.
- The public Career Coaches directory groups the 32 FinEx coach profiles by finance expertise and offers a direct appointment action for each profile.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is FinEx Careers, a Financial Executive Club platform. Existing public terminology includes Seeker, Role, Careers, Consultation, and Learning.

## Product Principles

- Make time-sensitive application information scannable.
- Distinguish verified facts from prototype or pending content.
- Keep the public discovery path direct and ungated.
- Preserve the portal's three established pillars while making graduate opportunities easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public surfaces must remain keyboard accessible, responsive down to small mobile screens, and meet WCAG AA contrast expectations. Deadline meaning must never depend on colour alone.
