# Hong Kong insurance product development: monthly market evidence

**Purpose.** Replace the legacy Hays total-package proxy ladder for
`insurance / product_development`. Researched 26 August 2026. Amounts are
monthly HKD.

## Direct monthly evidence and decision

The Government of Hong Kong's Talent Engage salary index publishes a direct
monthly ladder for Insurance Product Management Professionals:

| Published title | Monthly salary | Anchor decision |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Assistant Product Manager | 38–55k | Assistant Manager: 38–55k |
| Product Manager | 50–68k | Product Manager: 50–68k |
| Senior Product Manager | 75–95k | Senior Manager: 75–95k |
| Product Director | 120–180k | Lower part of combined Director / Head cell |
| Head of Product Development | 180–230k | Upper part of combined Director / Head cell |

The board retains 120–230k for its existing *combined* `Director / Head of
Products` schema coordinate. That width is intentional: a Product Director
and the function head are not equivalent jobs. A future schema split should
publish the two direct ranges independently.

Hays Insurance Product Development is retained only as a seniority sanity
check because Hays describes the annual values as total package—not fixed base;
no `/12`, `/13` or `/14` conversion was used for the above ranges. A current
Robert Walters Hong Kong Product Development Director advert at 100–120k is a
useful lower-end, role-specific cross-check, but the government index supplies
the primary title-for-title series.
