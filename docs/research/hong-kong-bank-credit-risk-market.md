# Hong Kong banking credit-risk salary market

**Purpose.** Close the `middle_office / risk_credit` market-evidence gap for Hong Kong *banking* roles, without treating compensation-package figures as base salary. Researched 26 August 2026. All money below is HKD.

## Executive conclusion

Credit risk is a distinct, well-paid middle-office speciality, but it is not a single title/grade. The defensible default should be a **banking credit-risk ladder**, with a separate override for highly specialised counterparty, markets/derivatives, modelling, or explicitly front-office-credit work.

The strongest clean base-pay sources put a normal bank credit-risk path at:

| Normalised anchor coordinate | Conservative monthly **base** recommendation | Evidence strength |
|---|---:|---|
| Analyst / Credit Analyst | 25–40k | Strong for 0–3 years (Randstad; PERSOLKELLY is slightly lower) |
| Associate | 35–55k | Moderate–strong for 3–6 years; broad title variance means not a narrow point |
| AVP / Manager | 40–60k | Moderate; current direct AVP benchmark is 36.7–46.7k, while experienced manager adverts reach 50–55k |
| Senior Manager / VP | 65–100k | Strong across the PERSOLKELLY and Robert Half management ladders |
| Associate Director | 80–120k | Moderate interpolation only: there is no clean, direct AD observation |
| Director | 100–150k | Moderate: starts at the 10+ years/base-pay evidence; do not infer from Hays' package number alone |
| Head of Credit Risk | 100–150k | Moderate for a normal bank HOD; preserve 120k+ for a true regional/group/CRO-1 leader or explicit large-team remit |

These are **base-salary defaults**, not a promise that every job title has a single value. A counterparty-credit/derivatives manager, a credit-modelling specialist, a regional role, and a retail credit-policy manager should remain eligible for a description-led or company-segment adjustment. Temporary roles must not reset permanent anchor values.

## What the local guides actually say

### Hays Asia Salary Guide 2026 — useful only as a package guardrail

The local source PDF includes two duplicate Hong Kong credit-risk tables (Audit, Risk & Compliance p.42; Banking/Middle Office p.69). The relevant Banking/Middle Office table is:

| Grade | Hong Kong annual figure printed by Hays (HK$000) |
|---|---:|
| Analyst | 240–480 |
| Associate | 420–720 |
| AVP | 600–900 |
| VP | 900–1,200 |
| Director | 1,200+ |
| Head of Department | 1,200–1,800 |

The page footnote explicitly says these are **representative of total annual package value**. They cannot be converted mechanically to monthly base: a bonus, allowances and guaranteed additional months are not identified. `/12` would produce an annualised package average, not base; `/13` or `/14` would be an even less supportable assumption. Use Hays to verify seniority order and to reject implausibly high or low proposals; do not source the anchor cells from it. Source: [Hays Asia Salary Guide 2026, p.69](../../salary_guidlines/2026%20Hays%20Asia%20Salary%20Guide%20HK%20%281%29.pdf).

### PERSOLKELLY Hong Kong Salary Guide 2025 — direct annual base salary

The guide's Banking & Financial Services page labels the column **“Range of Annual Base Salary (HKD)”** and its footnote says the figures are *not inclusive of benefits and bonuses*. Therefore `/12`, rather than `/13` or `/14`, is the appropriate normalisation. It gives the following broader **Risk Management** ladder (not credit-risk-specific):

| Experience / printed grade | Annual base | Monthly base after `/12` |
|---|---:|---:|
| Analyst, 0–3 years | 240–480k | 20–40k |
| Associate, 3–7 years | 480–720k | 40–60k |
| VP, 7–10 years | 720k–1.0m | 60–83.3k |
| Director, 10+ years | 1.0–1.5m | 83.3–125k |
| MD, 15+ years | 1.5m+ | 125k+ |

Source: [PERSOLKELLY Hong Kong Salary Guide 2025, p.16](../../salary_guidlines/1734427316-final_hk-salary-guide-2025_singlepage.pdf).

This is a strong *base-pay* and seniority bridge, but it should not be treated as a literal credit-risk table: it is the Risk Management category.

### Adecco Hong Kong Salary Guide 2026 — no applicable credit-risk table

The local guide is printed as monthly salary, but covers accounting/finance, corporate support, IT, legal/compliance and marketing. It contains IT risk and compliance roles, not banking credit risk. It is therefore **not evidence for this anchor** and should not be used merely because it already uses monthly figures. Source: [Adecco Hong Kong Salary Guide 2026](../../salary_guidlines/044fa74e-de92-456e-b352-a498fecd27fa.pdf).

## Independent Hong Kong market evidence

### Randstad HK 2025 — strongest credit-risk-specific base-pay comparator

Randstad's Banking & Financial Services / Middle Office table lists **Credit Risk** at 25–40k (1–3 years), 40–55k (3–6 years), 55–70k (7–10 years), and 100k+ (10+ years). The report describes its figures as permanent **basic monthly salary**, excluding AWS and fixed/variable bonus. This is the cleanest credit-risk-specific source for the lower and mid-career path, and it supports the proposed Analyst, Associate and VP floor. Source: [Randstad Hong Kong 2025 Job Market Outlook & Salary Report](https://www.randstad.com.hk/s3fs-media/hk/public/2025-01/RandstadHongKong-2025-Job-Market-Outlok-Salary-Report.pdf?VersionId=PHRexGlGGgtnsZybCcfOBtK7S.89J1E7).

### Robert Half HK 2026 — direct title mapping, base pay excluding extras

Robert Half's Hong Kong salary pages call the figures **gross yearly starting salaries**, explicitly excluding bonuses, benefits and MPF, and show the 25th, 50th and 75th percentiles. `/12` is the correct conversion:

| Published role | Annual starting salary, p25–p75 | Monthly base after `/12` | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Credit Risk Associate | 250–360k | 20.8–30k | Lower-bound comparator; its generic Associate title is less senior than many bank Associate postings |
| Credit Risk AVP | 440–560k | 36.7–46.7k | Direct AVP check |
| Senior Credit Risk Manager | 790k–1.21m | 65.8–100.8k | Direct Senior Manager / VP check |
| Credit Risk SVP | 1.42–1.913m | 118.3–159.4k | Upper leadership comparator, not ordinary VP |
| Head of Credit Risk | 1.42–1.913m | 118.3–159.4k | Large-company/upper-HOD comparator |

Sources: [Credit Risk Associate (Banking)](https://www.roberthalf.com/hk/en/job-details/credit-risk-associate-banking) and [Credit Risk AVP (Banking)](https://www.roberthalf.com/hk/en/job-details/credit-risk-avp-banking).

The Associate figure conflicts with Randstad/PERSOLKELLY. This is not a reason to average the three mechanically: Robert Half defines the 25th percentile as someone new to the role, whereas the board's “Associate” titles often imply 3–6 years or a bank grade. It should act as the lower boundary, not pull a bank Associate anchor down to 21–30k.

## Implications for the current board

There are 33 active primary listings whose title contains `credit risk`, including three common patterns that must not collapse into one grade:

1. **Slash/mixed grade titles**, particularly Bank of China (Hong Kong)'s Assistant/Deputy/Credit Risk Manager variants and China CITIC's Senior Manager/AVP. These need the existing mixed-title wide-range handling.
2. **Large global-bank functional grades**, such as JPMorgan/Morgan Stanley Credit Risk VP and Associate. Here the grade is meaningful, but desk scope (hedge funds, financial institutions, CCPs, funds) can justify a specific premium.
3. **True leadership**, such as BOCHK Deputy General Manager and CITIC Assistant General Manager. These are not ordinary Manager cells, even if their title includes credit risk.

Recommended implementation policy when this research is promoted to anchors:

1. Replace the generic `risk_credit` table with the conservative base-pay ladder in the executive conclusion, preserving a sufficiently broad range.
2. Let disclosed salaries, admin pins, named company policies and explicit multi-grade rules outrank it.
3. Do **not** treat the Hays figures as base `/12`, `/13` or `/14` evidence.
4. Keep `counterparty`, `derivatives`, `CCP`, `hedge fund`, `funds`, `financial institutions`, `model`, `portfolio`, `regional`, `global`, and `head/GM` as review/exception triggers until enough directly comparable base-pay observations exist.
5. Keep credit risk separate from market risk, operational risk, IT risk, insurance risk, compliance, and financial crime.

## Evidence quality and limitations

- **High confidence:** Randstad (credit-risk-specific, permanent monthly base), PERSOLKELLY (annual base with explicit exclusions), Robert Half (title-specific annual salary with explicit exclusions).
- **Medium confidence:** live recruiter adverts. They are current and useful for scope, but not all say whether the number excludes bonus/benefits and several are contract roles.
- **Do not use as base evidence:** Hays 2026 credit-risk table. It is highly credible for market seniority but expressly says *total annual package*.
- There is no clean direct Hong Kong observation for a banking Credit Risk **Associate Director**. Its proposed anchor is a transparent interpolation, not false precision.
