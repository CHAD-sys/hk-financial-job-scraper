# Hong Kong professional-practice / advisory: base-pay market evidence

**Purpose.** Find independent Hong Kong **base-salary** evidence for the
generic `professional_practice_advisory` ladder and the Big Four cohort (EY,
KPMG, Deloitte and PwC). Researched 26 August 2026. This is a research note
only: it does not amend an anchor, provenance record, resolver, database or
test.

## Short conclusion

There is good public recruiter-guide evidence for an adjacent Hong Kong
corporate-finance / audit / risk ladder on a genuine base-pay basis. It
supports the *ordering* and broad market envelopes for Analyst/Associate,
Manager, Senior Manager and Director. It is not firm-specific Big Four pay.
Current recruiter adverts which welcome Big Four candidates are useful
cross-checks, but they price an **exit role**, not employment at a Big Four
firm, and mostly do not define whether the displayed salary excludes bonus and
benefits.

There is therefore no defensible public source in this review for a universal
Big Four Partner base-salary maximum, or for treating `Director / Partner` as
one closed base-pay cell. Retain those as a market-evidence gap unless the
board obtains firm-disclosed fixed-pay data or a recruiter advert that both
names the employer/grade and explicitly states base pay.

## Grade-by-grade assessment

| Board-like grade | Evidence strength | Evidence-supported statement |
|---|---|---|
| Consultant / Associate | Moderate for a generic finance/advisory comparator; weak for Big Four-specific pay | Randstad and PERSOLKELLY Analyst rows support an adjacent basic-base range of roughly 25–40k. Do not present that as a Big Four Consultant series. |
| Senior Associate / Assistant Manager | Weak-to-moderate | The guides provide Manager/analyst ladders but no clean matching professional-practice grade. Current 40–70k IPO Associate adverts are exit-market observations, not an anchor. |
| Manager | Strong for generic corporate-finance/advisory, weak for firm-specific Big Four | Independent base guides cluster at 45–60k (Randstad) and 50–60k (PERSOLKELLY) for comparable work. The 50–70k current Michael Page role is corroborative but contract/exit-market evidence. |
| Senior Manager | Moderate | Randstad gives 60–86k for corporate-finance Senior Manager; PERSOLKELLY has 58.3–75k Senior Finance Manager and 70–83.3k Head of Planning & Analysis. This supports seniority ordering, not a firm-specific assignment. |
| Associate Director | Weak | No clean, employer-specific Big Four base series was found. An 80–90k Big Four Deal Advisory exit advert is insufficient because the employer, scope and pay basis differ. |
| Director | Moderate for generic corporate-finance leadership; weak for Big Four firm pay | Randstad's 85–120k corporate-finance Director is direct monthly base. PERSOLKELLY's 100–166.7k corporate Director/Department Head shows scope sensitivity. Keep Director separate from Partner. |
| Partner | Insufficient | Neither independent base guide publishes a comparable Partner row. Hays' `Director / Partner` is open-ended **total package**, so it cannot close a Partner base band. |

## Safe implications for a later anchor decision

1. Use Randstad and PERSOLKELLY as independent **base** sources for generic
   corporate-finance/audit/risk comparators. Preserve their labels and avoid
   pretending they identify EY, KPMG, Deloitte or PwC.
2. Treat Consultant/Associate, Manager, Senior Manager and Director as
   separate decisions. The published generic base rows support their order;
   they do not justify collapsing Director and Partner.
3. Keep the exact Hays tables as an explicitly labelled package-only sanity
   envelope. Do not use `/12`, `/13` or `/14` as a purported base conversion.
4. Do not derive a Partner cap from generic Director, MD or Hays package rows.
   A named-firm fixed-pay disclosure, a clearly base-only recruiter advert for
   that grade, or a licence-compliant compensation dataset would be needed.
5. Record any later choice as a *generic professional-practice base
   comparator* unless the evidence actually names the Big Four employer and
   grade. Current Big Four-welcome postings evidence the destination market,
   not compensation while employed by the firm.

## Limitations and next evidence to seek

- The strong published base guides classify by functional labour market, not
  by accounting-firm employer. Advisory, audit, risk, tax, forensics and
  transaction services should not silently inherit one another's rate.
- Public adverts age quickly and advertised salary can be a budget rather than
  accepted pay. Re-check the live URL and publication date before using one in
  an anchor provenance record.
- The best missing evidence would be a 2025/2026 Hong Kong recruiter guide
  with a `Professional Practice` or `Big Four` salary table and an explicit
  base-pay definition, or a named-firm Hong Kong posting that states fixed
  monthly/annual base separately from bonus.
