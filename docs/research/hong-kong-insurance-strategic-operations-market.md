# Hong Kong insurance strategic-operations salary market

**Purpose.** Replace the `insurance / strategic_operations` anchors that are
currently Hays-package proxies with defensible Hong Kong **base-pay** defaults.
Researched 26 August 2026. All amounts below are HKD per month.

## Executive conclusion

`strategic_operations` is too broad to be treated as a homogeneous insurance
function. It presently catches corporate strategy, transformation and PMO,
routine operations, digital/AI delivery, corporate development, governance and
distribution roles. The anchor should set a conservative default for genuine
local strategy/transformation/operations work while title and employer-grade
rules remain authoritative for the rest.

| Normalised role | Recommended monthly base default | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Analyst | 25–40k | Business/operations analyst, junior project or optimisation work; not a trainee or intern. |
| Associate / Assistant Manager | 35–50k | Experienced individual contributor or first-line project/operations lead. |
| Manager | 45–65k | Normal local transformation, PMO, operational-excellence or business-strategy manager. |
| Senior Manager / VP-equivalent functional lead | 70–95k | Senior transformation or strategy leadership, normally 8–12 years and material delivery accountability. |
| Director | 90–130k | Local functional strategy/transformation director, without a separate insurer corporate-grade rule. |
| Head | 110–160k | Local function head. Regional/global/enterprise heads and COO/C-suite roles require their own scope/grade rule, not this cell. |

The recommendation deliberately **does not** claim that an insurer's `AVP` or
`VP` is a universal functional title. The board already has insurer
corporate-grade policies (including company-specific AVP ranges). Those should
win over this generic role ladder. In particular, do not lower a reviewed
FWD/Sun Life/Manulife corporate AVP by treating it as an ordinary Manager.

## Compensation semantics: no invented divisor

### Hays 2026: do not convert

Hays' Hong Kong guide has an insurance *Projects & Transformation* table (PDF
p.125)—Business Analyst 240–600k, Assistant Manager 400–600k, Project Manager
600–960k, Senior Project/Program Manager 960k–1.3m and Transformation Head
1.3–2.0m—and adjacent *Strategic* and *Operations* tables (PDF p.126). The
Strategic table prints Analyst 280–540k, Manager 600–800k, Associate
Director/Senior Manager 1.1–1.7m and Director/AVP 1.4–2.2m; the Operations
table prints Assistant Manager 420–540k, Manager 540–720k, Senior Manager
700–950k and COO 1.8–2.5m. These values are useful as a
seniority/order check only. Hays defines them as **total annual package**, not
base salary. A `/12` result is an average monthly package; `/13` or `/14`
invents a composition the publisher does not provide. It must not be used as a
source for monthly-base anchors. See [Hays Asia Salary Guide 2026](../../salary_guidlines/2026%20Hays%20Asia%20Salary%20Guide%20HK%20%281%29.pdf) and the project's
[compensation-semantics review](../salary_anchor_compensation_semantics_2026-08-21.md).

### PERSOLKELLY 2025: direct annual base, therefore `/12`

PERSOLKELLY's Insurance tables are printed as **annual base salary, excluding
benefits and bonuses**. `/12` is therefore a period conversion, not a package
assumption; the guide does not state the treatment of a 13th-month/AWS, so no
further divisor is justified.

There is no exact Strategic Operations row, so its closest insurance
comparators should be used as envelopes rather than mechanically blended:

| Closest PERSOLKELLY insurance comparator | Printed annual base | Monthly after `/12` | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Policy administration, Officer/Senior Analyst | 210–280k | 17.5–23.3k | Operational lower bound, too low for strategic analyst by itself. |
| Policy administration, Assistant Manager/Manager | 360–540k | 30–45k | Routine insurance-operations manager floor. |
| Policy administration, Senior Manager/Senior Director | 660–960k | 55–80k | Lower-scope senior-operations envelope. |
| Management reporting, Assistant Manager/Manager | 456–720k | 38–60k | Analytical/control manager comparator. |
| Management reporting, Senior Manager/Senior Director | 840k–1.6m | 70–133k | Broad senior leadership comparator, not a normal PMO default. |
| Finance operations, Assistant Manager/Manager | 420–660k | 35–55k | Operations manager comparator. |
| Finance operations, Senior Manager/Senior Director | 720k–1.0m | 60–83k | Lower-scope senior-operations comparator. |

Source: [PERSOLKELLY Hong Kong Salary Guide 2025, Insurance pp. 41–44](../../salary_guidlines/1734427316-final_hk-salary-guide-2025_singlepage.pdf).

### Adecco 2026: direct monthly, but only an adjacent role family

Adecco publishes monthly salaries that exclude overtime, commissions,
allowances and bonuses. Its IT table does not establish insurance pay, but is
a useful **delivery-role** sanity check: Project Manager 45–75k, PMO 55–90k,
IT Operations Manager 60–80k, Program Manager 75–110k, and Head of Technology
Transformation 100–200k. This supports the manager/senior transformation
envelope but should not price a non-technical insurer role on its own.
Source: [Adecco Hong Kong Salary Guide 2026](https://image.marketing.info.adecco.com/lib/fe32117175640474731478/m/1/044fa74e-de92-456e-b352-a498fecd27fa.pdf).

## Audit of the active title population

The live primary board has **67** listings presently classified
`insurance / strategic_operations`. That classification is a useful routing
bucket, but not a safe single anchor. The important splits are:

1. **In scope for the new default:** local transformation/strategy and PMO
   work—e.g. `Assistant Manager, Transformation Office & Strategic
   Operations`; `Manager, Project Management Office, Operation Excellence`;
   `Manager, Business Planning & Strategy`; `Senior Manager (Customer
   Transformation & Change Management)`; `Associate Director, Operations
   Transformation`; and `Director, Distribution Transformation`.
2. **Routine insurance operations—route to the function instead:** clinic,
   contact-centre, policy administration, claims, underwriting, treasury or
   agency-service operations. A word such as *operations* does not turn these
   into strategy/transformation roles. Their existing policy-administration,
   claims, underwriting or finance/operations anchors are more appropriate.
3. **Governance, corporate development, control and data/AI—do not blanket
   price:** `Head of Corporate Development and Governance Control`,
   `Assistant Vice President, Control & Governance`, `Associate Director,
   Data Analytics and AI`, and technology/core-system delivery need their
   corporate-grade, risk/governance, analytics or IT rule. Geographic scope
   (`Asia`, `APAC`, `Group`) is a further review trigger.
4. **Distribution/product/agency strategy—use the closer function:** agency
   strategy, product proposition, customer management, distribution
   transformation and bancassurance can carry commercial or product scope.
   The generic strategic-operations ladder is a fallback only when those
   closer signals are absent.
5. **Mixed titles and contract jobs:** `Manager/Assistant Manager` and
   `Director/Associate Director` must retain the existing broad mixed-grade
   treatment. A fixed-term label alone is not grounds to discount permanent
   salary anchors; it is merely weaker evidence for market calibration.

## Implementation guardrails

1. Replace the four non-executive Hays-derived Strategic Operations cells with
   the base-pay ladder above. The literal `COO` cell remains an explicit market
   gap: it cannot truthfully stand in for the local-function Head range, and
   there is not yet direct evidence for a Hong Kong insurer COO base anchor.
   Add a distinct Associate/Assistant Manager coordinate only if the anchor
   schema supports it without breaking mapping. Otherwise use the 35–50k band
   as a resolver-level clarification, not a made-up table row.
2. Keep explicit insurer AVP/VP/Director/Head corporate-grade rules,
   disclosed salaries, administrative pins, company-size policies and
   multi-grade ambiguity bands above this fallback.
3. Prevent `COO`, `Chief`, `regional`, `global`, `group`, `Asia` and `APAC`
   scope from being terminally resolved by this function. They require a
   separate grade/scope decision.
4. Do not replay all 67 listings when changing the coordinates. First audit
   which listings actually resolve to the affected function/grade; many are
   better served by a more specific existing role.

## Evidence confidence and limitations

- **High confidence on compensation semantics:** Hays is a total-package
  source; PERSOLKELLY is annual base; Adecco is monthly salary excluding the
  listed extras.
- **High confidence for Manager and Senior Manager strategy/transformation:**
  two current permanent Randstad adverts provide direct Hong Kong evidence.
- **Moderate confidence for Analyst, Associate/AM, Director and Head:** these
  are conservative bridges from explicit insurance base ladders plus the
  current observations. There is no clean, published, insurance-specific
  base-pay series for every Strategic Operations title.
- **No generic `/13` or `/14`:** none of the usable base-like sources tells us
  how AWS/13th-month is handled. Dividing PERSOLKELLY annual base by `/12` is
  correct for the table's monthly period; inventing an extra payment divisor
  would understate the anchor.
