# Hong Kong financial-markets sales: base-pay calibration

**Purpose.** Replace the legacy total-package proxy ladder for generic
`front_office / financial_markets_sales` (Associate, VP, Director and MD).
Researched 26 August 2026. Amounts below are monthly HKD.

## Evidence and decision

The local Hays financial-markets-sales table is useful only as a service-line
and seniority check: Hays defines its guide values as annual **total package**,
so neither `/12` nor `/13` or `/14` establishes base salary. The government
Hong Kong Talent Engage (HKTE) summary of the 2024 Financial Services
Compensation Survey separately reports Hong Kong **salary** and bonus by rank:
Analyst/Associate US$78,069, VP US$129,934, Director US$349,425 and MD
US$743,750 annually. It is a cross-finance-rank survey, not a
financial-markets-sales-only ladder, so it is an outer base-pay comparator,
not a literal title match.

The published generic ladder is therefore deliberately conservative and broad:

| Grade | Published range | Rationale |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Associate | 35–55k | Sits below a normal VP and avoids treating every markets-sales Associate as a private-bank or investment-banking Associate. |
| VP | 65–100k | Consistent with the HKTE all-finance VP salary midpoint; employer-specific VP rules may exceed it. |
| Director | 100–160k | A generic local Director fallback, kept far below the survey-wide senior-leader average where title scope is unknown. |
| MD | 150–250k | A wide fallback only; global, regional, revenue-owner and disclosed-pay roles must be priced by their more specific evidence. |

These are base-pay operating ranges, not a claim that the HKTE survey is a
financial-markets-sales table. The board's explicit JPMorgan/Citi and
smaller-bank sales rules remain more specific and win over this generic
fallback.

## Sources

- [Hong Kong Talent Engage — Hong Kong: A Financial Professional's
  Powerhouse](https://www.hkengage.gov.hk/en/essentials/career-business/hong-kong-a-financial-professionals-powerhouse),
  accessed 26 August 2026. Its table distinguishes average salary from average
  bonus and total compensation, so only the salary column is used as a
  base-like cross-check.
- [Hays Hong Kong Salary Guide methodology](https://www.hays.com.hk/salary-guide),
  accessed 26 August 2026. Confirms Hays guide values are total annual package,
  not a fixed-base series.

## Guardrails

- Never flatten a stated AVP/ED, regional/global, desk-head, or revenue/P&L
  role into these generic corporate labels.
- Do not use this range for relationship management, investment banking,
  research, trading, or product roles merely because a listing mentions markets.
- Preserve disclosed salary and employer-specific rules before this fallback.
